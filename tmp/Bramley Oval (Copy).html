    How it’s drawn

    The ground is real 3D geometry, rendered small (the pixel size slider sets how small), outlined wherever depth jumps, then snapped to one fixed palette of 90 colours. Nothing is a sprite, and nothing is downloaded: the sound is synthesised in your browser.

    Clubs and players are invented. The ball is simulated: bounce, turn, edges and fielders reaching it are all computed, and every run, catch and run-out comes from where the ball and the batters actually are. Reviews work on the real ball track.
